Privacy Policy

Mohit Public Data Research | Effective 27 August 2026

Purpose

Mohit Public Data Research is a research and analysis application operated by Mohit Silla and WNE3 Technologies. It helps authorized users study public advertising, public business profiles, public social content, and campaign-research signals.

Information processed

The application may process public Facebook Page and Instagram professional-account metadata, public posts and media, public advertising information, and information from accounts that an authorized user explicitly connects. It may also process account identifiers, access tokens, and API request logs needed to operate and secure the service.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide research, reporting, account-authorized analysis, troubleshooting, and security. The application does not sell personal information or use connected-account information for unrelated advertising.

Sharing and retention

Information is shared only with service providers required to operate the application, when an authorized user requests an integration, or when required by law. Access credentials are kept restricted and retained only while needed for the connected service. Research outputs and operational logs are deleted when no longer needed.

User choices

An authorized user may revoke Meta permissions through their Facebook or Instagram settings. Requests to access or delete application-held information can be made using the data deletion instructions.

Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect credentials and connected-account information. No Internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.
